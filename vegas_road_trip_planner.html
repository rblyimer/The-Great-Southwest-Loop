Sat · Jul 25
Zion → Las Vegas → Fly Home
✈ Late flight home
Zion + Finale ▾
Start Zion early — shuttles and trails fill fast. Leave Springdale by 4–5 PM Mountain Time. You gain an hour entering Pacific Time heading to Vegas. Book a late LAS flight, ideally 10 PM or later.
Must-See in Zion
  • 🌊 The Narrows — wading up the Virgin River slot canyon (PERFECT in July heat)
  • Riverside Walk
  • Emerald Pools
  • Court of the Patriarchs viewpoint
  • Springdale views from town
Optional
  • Canyon Overlook Trail (if timing works)
  • Zion Human History Museum
Skip (unless planned ahead)
  • Angel's Landing — requires permit lottery, don't attempt without one
  • Long hikes given the flight timing
Final Timing: Dawn at Zion → hike until early afternoon → depart ~4–5 PM MT → arrive Las Vegas ~6–7 PM PT → late-night flight home 🎉
